Fila 0.5.11 makes updating and removing the package more dependable.

- An update now stops the background service wherever the system had placed it before starting the new one. On RootHide the old one could stay behind and keep Fila from reaching full access until a restart.
- Removing Fila stops the service even when the removal was interrupted.
- Fila now reliably notices that it was updated while open, including an update that lands in the moment it starts.
