Fila 0.6.3 takes the star off the breadcrumb.

- The current folder's name in the breadcrumb no longer has a star after it. Tapping it still adds it to or removes it from Favorites and lists every favourite, and holding it still jumps to one of the Places.

Choose the RootHide or the rootless Debian package for a jailbroken device, the TrollStore TIPA for TrollStore, or the standard IPA to sideload. SHA-256 checksums are in `SHA256SUMS`.
